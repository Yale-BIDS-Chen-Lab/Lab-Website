# Teams

We are a growing interdisciplinary lab working across biomedical NLP, medical LLMs, multimodal AI, medical imaging, and trustworthy AI in medicine. Our team brings together expertise from informatics, computer science, and clinical medicine. We are fortunate to work with talented trainees and collaborators at different stages, and we are proud to support their growth.

</section>

## Interested in Joining the Lab?

Our lab aims to support the growth of independent researchers through regular communication, thoughtful feedback, research rigor, and increasing independence over time. We work with trainees at different stages, including postdoctoral fellows, PhD students, medical trainees, interns, and research assistants.

Qingyu Chen has received the NIH Summer Mentor Award four times and the NLM Data Science and Informatics Mentoring Award.

Please visit the [**Join**](/join) page for current opportunities and guidance on how to reach out.
